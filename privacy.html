3 Categories of Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data strictly necessary to deliver bookkeeping, cloud synchronization, and offline financial management services.

A. Account & Registration Data

  • Mobile Phone Number: Required for account creation, phone OTP verification, PIN recovery, and cloud restoration across devices.
  • Business Profile: Merchant name, shop/store name, optional business category, and registered store address.
  • Security Credentials: A salted cryptographic PIN hash (your actual 4-digit PIN is never stored in plain text).

B. Ledger & Accounting Transaction Records

  • Customer / Supplier Identity: Name and mobile phone number manually entered or selected by the merchant to label ledger accounts.
  • Financial Transaction Details: Monetary amount (Got / Gave), date and time of transaction, transaction status (Active, Cancelled), payment mode (Cash, Online, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque), and optional descriptive notes.
  • Cashbook & Job Diary Entries: Daily cash in/out entries and karigar/job workshop progress records created by the merchant.

C. User-Generated Media & Documents (Optional)

  • Bill & Receipt Photos: Compressed digital images (e.g., invoices, bills, physical ledger vouchers) captured by the user to substantiate transactions.
  • Voice Memos: Short audio notes recorded by the user to attach spoken details to specific entries.
  • PDF Documents: Invoice or payment slips attached to transaction records.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Telemetry (PII-Masked)

  • Device Metadata: Device model, operating system version (e.g., Android 14 / iOS 17), application build number (v1.0.5), language setting, and network connectivity status.
  • Crash Incident Logs: Automatic crash reporting to rectify bugs. All diagnostic dumps strictly pass through our PII & Telemetry Masking Engine, which automatically redacts phone numbers (e.g., +91 98****1234), OTPs, and authentication tokens before any telemetry is recorded.

8 Encryption & Technical Security Architecture

KhataRecord is engineered with defense-in-depth principles to protect financial ledgers against unauthorized inspection, data leaks, and malicious tampering:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3): All network communications between your mobile device, browser, and our cloud infrastructure are strictly forced over HTTPS with modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic cipher suites.
  • PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS): Every database table enforces cryptographic tenant isolation. A merchant can only execute queries against rows explicitly matching their verified authentication identifier. Cross-tenant reads or modifications are blocked at the database engine level.
  • Zero-Hardcoding Guarantee: No backend master passwords, private API secret keys, or administrative tokens are ever baked into mobile APK/AAB distribution binaries.
  • PIN Brute-Force Defense: Merchant security PINs are protected by automatic rate-limiting and temporary account lockout triggers after sequential failed attempts, defending against automated dictionary or brute-force attacks.
  • MobSF SAST Security Certification & OWASP MSTG Compliance: KhataRecord undergoes rigorous Static Application Security Testing via Mobile Security Framework (MobSF v4.6.3). The release binary is verified with 0 High-severity vulnerabilities, 0 third-party advertising or analytics trackers, strictly sandboxed internal cacheDir storage, and hardened Android intent-filter validation.
  • Operating System Sandboxing: On Android and iOS, local SQLite databases and media caches are kept inside private app sandbox directories, inaccessible to other third-party apps installed on the device.

9 Offline-First Architecture & Local Device Storage

KhataRecord is built for real-world business resilience in markets and bazaars with spotty network coverage:

  • All transactions, customer profiles, and entries are committed immediately to your local device cache (SQLite / IndexedDB) in 0 milliseconds.
  • When an active internet connection (Wi-Fi, 4G, or 5G) is detected, our background synchronization engine securely pushes queued changes to Supabase Cloud.
  • If you choose to use KhataRecord entirely in offline mode without logging into an account, all records remain stored exclusively on your local physical device.

11 User Account & Data Deletion (Play Store & App Store Requirement)

In full compliance with Google Play's Data Deletion Policy and Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), we provide both instant in-app self-service deletion and an online web-based request mechanism to permanently delete your account and all associated ledger records.

Method A: Instant In-App Deletion (Self-Service)

You can purge your data immediately from inside the KhataRecord application:

  1. Open KhataRecord on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings (सेटिंग्स) icon in the top navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone (खतरे का क्षेत्र) section.
  4. Select व्यापार हटाएं (Delete Business) or खाता मिटाएं (Wipe Ledger / Account).
  5. Confirm your action. Your local database is wiped immediately, and an authenticated deletion cascade purges all customer accounts, transactions, and Cloudflare R2 media files from our cloud servers.

Method B: Web-Based Data Deletion Request (Mandatory Store URL)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your phone, you can request full account and data deletion by submitting the verified deletion request below. KhataRecord allows merchants to share bill receipts, transaction confirmation slips, and account statements directly with customers via WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business:
